# Wishlist & re-reads

[← README](../README.md)

## Wishlist

*Books → Wishlist*. Books you would like to have – they are **not part of the collection**: they don't
appear in the book list, on shelves, under unread/reading/read or in the statistics.

- **Add a wish**: *Wish* on the wishlist page (or type *Wishlist* in the book form), with ISBN lookup
  as usual. A book of the catalogue – e.g. a returned library loan you want to own – goes on the list
  with *Add to my wishlist* in its book view.
- **Bought / borrowed**: one tap turns the wish into an own book (received today, then pick its place)
  or a library loan (default loan period, otherwise 3 weeks). Scanning it into a shelf in batch mode, or
  adding the same ISBN normally, does the same.
- **Several people** can wish for the same book; once it is in the collection, all those wishes are done.

### Who sees it

Every list is **private** by default. On the wishlist page you choose:

| Setting | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| Only me | nobody else – a private wish does not even show up when someone scans that ISBN |
| Everybody here | everybody with an account on this instance (*Other people's wishlists*) |
| Anyone with the link | additionally everybody who has the link, **without signing in** – e.g. to share gift ideas |

The public page (`#/w/<token>`) only shows title, author, publisher, year, ISBN and cover – no reading
data, notes or names other than your username. *Create a new link* invalidates the old one; switching
back to *Only me* switches the link off.

## Reading a book more than once

In the book view:

- **Read again** – for a book marked as read: the finished read is kept, a new one starts today.
- **Add a read** – enter an earlier read afterwards (start optional, end required).

Statistics count **every finished read**: books and pages per month and year and the reading pace.
“Read” in the status overview still counts each book once. Earlier reads are exported in the CSV column
`Frühere Lesedurchgänge` and come back with an import.
